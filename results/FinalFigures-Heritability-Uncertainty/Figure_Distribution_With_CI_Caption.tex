\caption{\textbf{Distribution and uncertainty of SNP-heritability estimates across method families.} (A) Distribution of configuration-level SNP-heritability estimates for each method family. Binary phenotypes are shown on the liability scale, whereas body mass index is shown on its native quantitative scale. Boxes represent the interquartile range, central lines indicate the median, and whiskers extend to 1.5$\times$IQR; estimates below zero and above one were retained without truncation. (B) The same configuration-level estimates shown individually and coloured by phenotype, illustrating phenotype-specific contributions to the observed variation. (C) Distribution of estimator-specific 95\% confidence-interval widths for configuration--phenotype estimates with available estimator uncertainty. Binary-trait confidence intervals are shown on the liability scale and body mass index on its native quantitative scale. The percentage of estimates with available estimator-specific confidence intervals is shown for each method family. Configurations for which estimator-specific confidence intervals were unavailable were not assigned artificial uncertainty values. Cross-fold SD is reported separately as a descriptive stability measure and is not interpreted as an inferential standard error. Complete observed- and liability-scale binary estimates and their corresponding uncertainty measures are reported in Supplementary Table~S1.}
