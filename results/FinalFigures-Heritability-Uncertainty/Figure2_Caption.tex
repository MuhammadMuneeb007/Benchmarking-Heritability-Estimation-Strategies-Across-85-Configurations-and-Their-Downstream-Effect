\caption{\textbf{Distribution and cross-fold stability of empirical SNP-heritability estimates across method families.} (A) Distribution of configuration-level SNP-heritability estimates for each method family. Binary phenotypes are shown on the liability scale and body mass index on its native quantitative scale. Boxes represent the interquartile range, central lines indicate the median, and whiskers extend to 1.5$\times$IQR; estimates below zero and above one were retained without truncation. (B) The same configuration-level estimates shown individually and coloured by phenotype. (C) Cross-fold standard deviation of the configuration-level heritability estimates across the five training partitions, shown as a common descriptive stability measure for all method families, including LDpred2. Because the cross-validation training partitions overlap substantially, cross-fold SD is not interpreted as an estimator-specific standard error or confidence interval. Estimator-specific standard errors and 95\% confidence intervals, where available, together with complete observed- and liability-scale binary estimates, are reported in Supplementary Table~S1.}
