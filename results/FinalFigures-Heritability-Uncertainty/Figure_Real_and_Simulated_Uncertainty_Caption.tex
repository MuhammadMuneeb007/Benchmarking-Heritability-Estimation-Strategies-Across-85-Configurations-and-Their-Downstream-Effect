\caption{\textbf{Distribution, estimator-specific uncertainty, and cross-fold stability of SNP-heritability estimates in the empirical and simulated benchmarks.} (A) Distribution of configuration-level SNP-heritability estimates across method families in the empirical benchmark. Binary phenotypes are shown on the liability scale and body mass index on its native quantitative scale. (B) The same empirical estimates shown individually and coloured by phenotype. (C) Estimator-specific 95\% confidence-interval widths are shown where the underlying estimator reported valid uncertainty, alongside descriptive cross-fold 95\% stability widths derived from variation across the overlapping training folds. The latter are included to provide a common descriptive stability measure for methods without estimator-specific confidence intervals and must not be interpreted as inferential confidence intervals. (D) Descriptive cross-fold 95\% stability widths for simulated continuous traits at the two target sample sizes. (E) Corresponding stability widths for simulated binary traits on the liability scale. Complete observed- and liability-scale empirical binary estimates, estimator-specific uncertainty where available, and cross-fold stability measures are reported in Supplementary Table~S1.}
